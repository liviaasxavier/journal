# journal

A little digital corner of my own, with a bullet journal and scrapbook feel:
pink paper, washi tape, torn edges, wax seals and handwritten notes.

It brings my everyday life together in one place and syncs between my computer and my phone:

- **Everyday:** daily dashboard, color-coded calendar, to-do lists,
  habit tracker with charts and a diary with mood tracking
- **Studies:** college dashboard (grades, absences, schedule and exams),
  study topics and a Markdown notebook
- **Life:** goals with steps, idea bank, wishlist, recipes,
  books, movies and shows, and saved links
- **Stickers:** upload PNGs and stick them on any page, then drag, rotate and resize them

Built with **React + Vite** and **Supabase** (database, auth and storage), secured
with RLS so each user can only access their own data. Icons: Uicons by Flaticon.
